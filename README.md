# Pokémon X & Y Legacy

XY Legacy is the project we never really thought we would make, and we’re incredibly glad we did. The Legacy project originally began with a much smaller ambition: a ROM hack of TheSmithPlays’ favorite Pokémon game, Pokémon Crystal.

That idea grew into something much bigger, eventually giving us a trilogy with Yellow Legacy, Crystal Legacy, and Emerald Legacy. What we never expected was that Legacy would jump so far forward into the Nintendo 3DS era. That brings us to Pokémon X and Y: games packed with ideas, style, and potential, but also games that have long been criticized for their low difficulty, uneven balance, and missed opportunities. What began as a relatively straightforward rebalancing project quickly evolved into something far more ambitious. Kalos has a fantastic aesthetic, Mega Evolution remains one of the coolest mechanics the series has introduced, and Generation 6 brought new technology and systems that give these games an identity unlike anything that came before them.

The game has been reworked from top to bottom, resulting in some of the most exciting battles we have created to date. The progression and level curve have been completely rebalanced so that the EXP. All can function as an intentional, balanced part of the experience rather than simply trivializing it. The Pokédex has been redistributed and Pokémon have been rebalanced across the board, giving far more of the roster a meaningful opportunity to shine. X/Y Legacy is not intended to be a Kaizo-style difficulty hack. Expect ability and move improvements, expanded story content, a rebalanced Battle Chateau, a revamped Battle Maison, full Pokédex completion, improvements to the overall pacing and flow of the adventure, and plenty of postgame secrets and Easter eggs to discover. This is X and Y perfected in it's own vision with 10+ years of retrospect.

We sincerely hope you enjoy X/Y Legacy. Whether you have been with the Legacy project from the beginning or this is your very first adventure with us, we are incredibly grateful that you are here. The community is the reason we continue making these games. Seeing people rediscover a game they love, experience it in a new way, and feel that familiar hit of nostalgia is what makes all of the work worthwhile. Thank you for playing, and thank you for supporting the Legacy Team.


## Download and Play

* **Recommended:** Patch the game on the [XY Legacy Download Page](https://pokemonlegacy.com/xy-legacy/download). It also has setup instructions for each platform.
* **Alternative:** Download the patches zip for your game from [RELEASES](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Pokemon_XY_Legacy/releases/latest) and apply it with the [Legacy Patcher](https://github.com/cRz-Shadows/Legacy_Patcher).
* You need a clean, decrypted US base-game Pokémon X or Pokémon Y ROM with no game update applied.
